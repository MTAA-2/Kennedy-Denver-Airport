The unpaid time

For many airport workers, the workday starts before the time clock does. Three steps might add time to every shift:

  1. 1Waiting in the security lineStanding in the TSA line with your badge before work.
  2. 2Passing through screeningBags, scanners, and any secondary checks.
  3. 3Getting to your postWalking or taking the train or shuttle from security to where you clock in.

Colorado law might treat security screening as paid time.
